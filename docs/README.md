# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Purpose
This documentation set provides a practical, end-to-end project management process for OctoAcme teams. Use it to align delivery, reduce handoff friction, and onboard new teammates quickly.

## Start here
- New to the process? Begin with the [Project Management Overview](./octoacme-project-management-overview.md).
- Then follow the lifecycle stages below based on where your project is right now.

## Lifecycle guide
1. **Initiation**  
   Validate the business need, align stakeholders, and define success criteria before committing delivery work.  
   Read: [Project Initiation](./octoacme-project-initiation.md)

2. **Planning**  
   Break work into a prioritized backlog, set acceptance criteria, and agree on milestones, dependencies, and Definition of Done.  
   Read: [Project Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md)

3. **Execution & Tracking**  
   Run the team rhythm, manage pull requests and testing, and track progress and blockers through delivery.  
   Read: [Execution & Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md)

4. **Risk Management & Communication**  
   Maintain the risk register, send clear status updates, and use escalation paths when needed.  
   Read: [Risk Management & Communication](./octoacme-risks-and-communication.md)

5. **Release & Deployment**  
   Prepare releases with checklist discipline, verify deployments, and communicate outcomes.  
   Read: [Release & Deployment](./octoacme-release-and-deployment.md)

6. **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement**  
   Capture learnings, assign action items, and feed improvements into future work.  
   Read: [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md)

7. **Roles & Personas (cross-cutting)**  
   Use shared role definitions to clarify responsibilities and improve collaboration across all stages.  
   Read: [Roles & Personas](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md)
